02 Who I am

About me

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Universidad Pontificia de Salamanca. I currently work as a .NET Backend Developer at Mecalux, in the logistics sector, developing and maintaining backend solutions for warehouse management systems at real scale.

My professional goal is to grow into Software Architect or AI Engineer roles. To that end, I'm studying the AI-Powered Software Development Master's Degree (Máster de Desarrollo con IA) at Big School, aimed at both junior developers and professionals looking to integrate AI into their daily workflow and move toward AI-powered development roles.

Outside of code I'm a 2nd Dan Judo black belt and federated instructor. I've spent years training and leading groups of students — something that carries directly into how I work in a team: with consistency, method, and a real ability to teach what I know.
